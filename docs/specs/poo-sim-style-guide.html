Style Guide & Palette Lock v1 · LOCKED

The single source of truth for colour, type, shape, and tone. New art matches this; code migrates to it.

This isn't invented — it's locked from what already ships. Every colour below is lifted straight from the running prototype (push_prototype.gd, manual_overlay.gd, level_picker.gd), the build you've been playtesting. The job of this page is to give those values names and roles so nothing drifts, and to pin down the two visual registers, the type system, and the shape rules around them.

§1The two registers

Poo Sim is one joke told in two visual voices. Keep them distinct — the contrast is the comedy (spec §11).

A · Chunky Cartoon — the world

Bold, saturated, cel-shaded. Thick uniform outlines, flat fills, two-tone shading, exaggerated shapes. The characters, toilets, environments, hazards, victory bursts. Gross-out via cartoon exaggeration, never realism.

B · Deadpan Technical — the framing

Ink-on-paper engineering-manual clinical: thin linework, leader lines, small-caps mono labels, registration marks (this page, and FIG. 1). Used for the HUD's earnest gauges, the field manual, tutorials, loading plates. Played completely straight.

§4Typography PROPOSED

Two families, one per register. Free/OFL, embeddable in Godot later. (The prototype currently uses the engine fallback — these are the proposal to lock.)

Display — the cartoon UI

The Push — 100%

Fredoka (or Baloo 2 / Nunito ExtraBold). Chunky, rounded, friendly, high legibility on a phone. Titles, meter readouts, buttons, the big juicy numbers.

Technical — the manual voice

FIG.1 — THE OPERATOR

IBM Plex Mono (or Space Mono). Clinical, evenly-spaced, deadpan. All the field-manual copy, diagram labels, loading tips, achievement citations.

§5Shape language

  • Thick, uniform outlines on all cartoon shapes — ~0.5% of screen width, rounded caps & joins.
  • Flat fills + one shadow tone. Two-tone cel shading, never a smooth gradient in the base art.
  • Gradients & glow are juice only — the flow-zone pulse, a milestone flash — never structural.
  • Corner radius scale: 6 (chips) · 10 (panels) · pill = height/2 (meters, buttons).
  • Squiggle stink-lines & motion lines are the sanctioned comedic flourish.

§6Do & Don't

Do

  • Readability first. Meters & hazards must pop against any busy background (pillar 2).
  • Keep colour = meaning. Green is always go, red is always danger — everywhere, no exceptions.
  • Play the manual voice straight. The gravity is the gag.
  • Sell the gross-out with comedy & audio, not the visuals.

Don't

  • No photoreal / realistic grime. It kills the joke and the appeal.
  • Don't repurpose the semantic colours as decoration or theming.
  • No gradients in base shapes. Flat + 2-tone only.
  • Don't crowd the HUD. One decision at a time; art serves the read.
